Welcome to Al-Rahma Centre

Barawa Al-Rahma Islamic Centre is a Muslim organisation dedicated to empowering individuals and communities around London. We believe in creating positive change through education, community building, and humanitarian efforts. Join us in our mission to create a better place for all.

Our Mission

Our mission is to enrich the community by providing a center for Islamic worship, education, and engagement. We offer spiritual guidance, educational programs, and spaces for prayer and activities.

Vision

We aim to foster a thriving and inclusive Muslim community rooted in Islamic principles, promoting faith, education, and unity.

Projects

Teaching Quran

Discover a deeper connection to the Quran through our tailored programs led by knowledgeable teachers.

Community Services

Join us in building a compassionate community through programs like weekly Islamic lessons and Quran memorization.
